# rekha 1.1.2 — the P(-1) audit

**Audited:** rekha 1.1.1 (`src/` 13,175 lines), on cyrius 6.7.18, sadish 0.12.1 and the sankoch
2.8.9 in the 6.7.18 stdlib. **Reference:** FreeType 2.14.3's source; host FreeType 2.15.0 for one
unhinted-CFF comparison. **Plan:** [`docs/development/roadmap.md`](../development/roadmap.md),
*1.1.2 — the P(-1) audit*. **What a consumer sees:** CHANGELOG 1.1.2, which names every input
rekha now refuses that 1.1.1 accepted.

This is rekha's first audit directory. 0.3.11's audit, the one before it, is recorded in its
CHANGELOG entry.

## Method

0.3.11's standard of proof was applied:
- every finding is reproduced by a probe before it is fixed;
- every fix gets a test that is **mutation-checked**: with the fix removed, the test is seen to
  fail;
- every new refusal is swept against the host's fonts — 2,155 faces, every glyph, plus the 607
  WOFF2 files for H-3 — before it lands.

1. **Cleanliness (roadmap step 1).** At 1.1.1 every CI gate is green: `lint --strict`, formatting
   in place, `vet`, `distlib --check`, the API surface and private-bundle probes, zero-warning
   builds under both DCE modes, and the aarch64 and AGNOS links.
2. **Internal review (step 3).** One read-only adversarial lens per surface:
   - the hint machine, and hinted glyph loading;
   - CFF / CFF2;
   - the containers;
   - variations;
   - layout and names;
   - the boundary (`bounds`);
   - documented contracts;
   - the core (`sfnt` / `glyf` / `cmap`);
   - external research.

   Each finding was reproduced and then refuted by separate agents. ⚠ **The review was stopped
   part-way** (233 agents launched) as larger than the job needed. Its record:
   - 101 raw findings: 73 confirmed, 15 rejected, 13 not yet checked. Each of the 13 was
     reproduced here before it was fixed.
   - Two rounds ran for CFF, contracts, core, hinted loading and variations. One round ran for the
     containers, the boundary, the hint machine, layout and names, and research.
   - The *loop until dry* and *completeness critic* stages never ran.
   - The containers and boundary lenses proved nothing in their one round, but each left an
     unproven lead. The boundary's became hint-machine#1, rejected below. The containers' lead was
     checked in the dossier pass and is **H-3**, the audit's third HIGH.
3. **External research (step 4).** One round. It found H-1 on its own, and the WOFF2 size finding
   that was rejected and then reopened (L-39).
4. **Tools (step 6).** These cover the containers and variations the cut-short review reached
   least:
   - `programs/mutate_test.cyr`: seven seeds built in code — CFF, CFF2 variable, `gvar` / HVAR /
     MVAR / GPOS with hinting, that face as a stored and a zlib WOFF, a transformed WOFF2, and a
     Brotli WOFF2 — with 1,000 mutants each and a sentinel gap after every table and pool block;
   - `poison_alloc` redzones through the `sd_alloc` seam;
   - a genuinely Brotli-compressed WOFF2 path in `programs/woff2_test.cyr`.

Finding IDs below are this dossier's. `lens#n` is the review record's own label for each raw
finding that a row merges.

## HIGH

| ID | Finding | Resolution | Proof |
|---|---|---|---|
| H-1 | **CFF / CFF2 FDSelect format 0 read past the table** (`src/cff.cyr` `rekha_cff_fd`; cff#1, research#1). The FD lookup ran BEFORE the gid was checked against CharStrings, and format 0 reads one byte per gid unbounded. `maxp` may declare more glyphs than the CFF holds, so a `cmap` can hand out an id in the gap — past the table, and past `len` for a large id. | Two layers. `rekha_cff_load` refuses a gid outside CharStrings as the TrueType path does (empty, `REKHA_ERR_OTHER` "glyph id"). The format 0 read is also bounded by the glyph count. | SIGSEGV reproduced before the fix. `cff_test`: both FDSelect formats at gid 4, 5, −1 and 1,000,000. Either guard removed fails the test. |
| H-2 | **STAT `elidedFallbackNameID` read past the table** (`src/stat.cyr`; contracts#1, layout-names#1). A minorVersion ≥ 1 header is 20 bytes, but 18 were required, so `rekha_stat_fallback_name_id` read 2 bytes past an 18-byte table — past `len` when STAT was last. | An 18- or 19-byte 1.1 table is the "header that does not fit" `docs/api/names.md` already promised, so STAT reads as absent. The reader also bounds the field itself. | SIGSEGV reproduced before the fix. `stat_test`: declared lengths 18, 19 and 20, and an 18-byte 1.0 table that stays valid. With both guards removed the test reads back `0xFFFF`. |
| H-3 | **WOFF2 `hmtx` transform reads a null-transformed `loca` past its end** (`src/woff2.cyr` `rekha_w2_xmin`; the containers lens's unproven lead, proven in the dossier pass). The transform rebuilds every omitted bearing from glyf's xMin through loca, for all `maxp` glyphs, and loca's length was never compared with them. The directory may lay loca out last, so the reads ran past the whole rebuilt file — the `sd_alloc(size)` `rekha_font_open_woff2` makes. | `rekha_w2_hmtx` refuses a loca shorter than numGlyphs + 1 entries (OpenType's own rule) in the measuring pass, so `rekha_woff2_sfnt_size` answers 0 and nothing is allocated. FreeType reads on into whatever follows (`get_x_mins`, sfwoff2.c). | Sentinel differential: a 125,752-byte rebuild decoded into a buffer whose tail was filled two ways — 60,004 bytes of the result changed with bytes the decoder does not own. `woff2_test` group R4: both loca formats, `ng + 1` entries decode identically under two fills, one entry short is refused. The guard removed, and moved one entry, both fail. The 607 WOFF2 files on the host decode byte-identically before and after. |

## Other measurements

- **Host sweeps.** 2,155 faces, every glyph, before and after each refusal landed:
  - the only glyph any rule refuses is U+E783 in the nine CodeNewRoman Nerd faces: 4,220
    points, over the 4,096 point cap. The roadmap's raisable-ceilings item records it;
  - the GPOS ceilings sit well above every host face: at most 12 kern lookups, 29 pairs walked
    and 19 subtables;
  - the 607 host WOFF2 files decode identically across H-3.
- **Hostile corpus.** `hostile_test`'s mutants decode 735 fewer outlines than at 1.1.1, and 2,614
  fewer hinted glyphs (238,294 hinted loads now). Every drop traces to an intended refusal: an
  unusable `head` (M-20) or `unitsPerEm` out of range (L-35). The A/B sentinel differential is 0.
- **`mutate_test`.** Seven seeds × 1,000 mutants in about 8.5 s, with a poisoned sweep of every
  mutant. Planted bugs it catches are listed in its header.
- **`poison_alloc`.** An outline block or a rebuilt WOFF2 one byte short — CFF, simple glyf,
  composite merge, WOFF2 — each stops the run with exit 86. `hostile_test` goes from 8.6 s to
  13.2 s with it.
- **`--bounds`** (cyrius 6.7.8) adds 40 bytes to every binary and no checks: rekha reads through
  `load8` .. `load64` on computed addresses, which the mode does not see. **No CI leg**; revisit
  if `src/` ever indexes a typed array by subscript.
- **`[test] stdlib`.** The harness's leaves moved into `cyrius.cyml`'s `[test]`:
  - `lib/` grew from 17 to 36 files, all hash-pinned in `cyrius.lock`;
  - every binary is byte-identical, and `dist/*.deps` is unchanged.
- **Scaffold.** The nine internals with no call site are removed: seven WOFF2 directory
  accessors, `rekha_hint_trig_atan` and `rekha_cff2_regions`.

## Not covered

- The review's *loop until dry* and *completeness critic* stages never ran. Several lenses had one
  round: the containers, the boundary, the hint machine, layout and names, and research. H-3
  stayed hidden behind one of those single rounds until the dossier pass checked its lead.
  `mutate_test`'s container seeds did not reach it: the reads stayed inside each seed's buffer,
  and poison redzones see writes, not reads. **The next audit should start with a second
  containers round and a layout round**, and give `mutate_test` a WOFF2 seed whose `hmtx` is
  transformed over a null-transformed glyf / loca.
- The divergences above, and those already in the roadmap's table, stand as recorded decisions
  for 1.x.
